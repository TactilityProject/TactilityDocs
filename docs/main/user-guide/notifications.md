# Notifications

Apps and services can tell you that something happened, for example that a mesh message arrived. Notifications are kept until you remove them. They are not saved when the device restarts.

## Showing them

- **Notifications button:** the system bars have a button with a bell. It only shows while there are notifications. It sits above the page buttons in the side layout, and next to them in the split layout. Press it to open the notifications. When a new notification arrives while the notifications are closed, the bell gets a dot. Opening the notifications turns it back into a plain bell. An update that renotifies (the mesh unread count, for example) does the same. A notification that arrives while they are open, or that is updated without renotifying, doesn't change it. It isn't remembered after a restart.
- **System menu:** swipe from the right half of the screen to open the Notifications part of the [system menu](navigation.md#system-menu).

Each notification shows the app or service that sent it in small text, then its title and message, and the time. A bar on its left edge sets it apart from the next one. Notifications that stay until what they report is resolved are always at the top of the list, and have a pin next to their time.

## Using them

- **Tap** a notification to open what it is about, for example a mesh conversation. A notification that you tapped is removed afterwards. Some notifications stay until the thing that they report is resolved (the mesh message count stays while there are unread messages).
- **Clear** removes the notifications that you can clear. It is the button at the right of the top row, and it is hidden when there are none. A notification that stays until what it reports is resolved (such as the mesh unread count) can't be cleared.
- Without a touchscreen, move through the list with the arrow keys and press **Enter** on a notification.

The 16 most recent notifications are kept. A new one removes the oldest when the list is full.

## Choosing who may notify

**Settings → Notifications** lists every app and service that has sent a notification. Turn one off to stop its notifications. That also removes the ones that are pending. The choice is kept after a restart. A new sender is on until you turn it off.

For mesh messages, you can also choose per conversation: open a contact or channel, open the menu and select **Notifications**. **All messages** raises a notification for new messages. **None** doesn't. Unread counts are kept either way.
